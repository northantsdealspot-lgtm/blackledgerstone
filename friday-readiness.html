Acceptance tests

Friday demonstration sequence

  1. Enter a natural-language property or land requirement.
  2. Show the research/source route and evidence status.
  3. Promote a genuine opportunity into the deal register.
  4. Run due diligence, planning/development and financial appraisal.
  5. Show risk/confidence and distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
  6. Run Raven Match + CAIT against the investor pool.
  7. Demonstrate the fee, POF and human-approval release gate.
  8. Export the backup and show recovery capability.
BlackLedgerStone rule: never fabricate a source result, operational status, investor qualification or planning conclusion.
