Primary business model

Source and package opportunities for investors, developers and operators. Avoid using personal capital wherever possible. Earn through sourcing, introductions, co-sourcing, development introductions and protected fee agreements.

Fastest-money priorities

Motivated vendors, public-sector disposals, receivership/administration, auction and failed-sale stock, land/development, C2/care, BRR and HMO where evidence supports margin.

Search territory

Northamptonshire first for local advantage, then UK-wide and Ireland-wide where the opportunity is strong enough. No artificial postcode ceiling.

Deal filter

Prefer clear discount, realistic investor demand, manageable due diligence and short path to fee. Reject weak margin, unverifiable claims and opportunities where risk overwhelms reward.

Evidence standard

Never turn an assumption into a fact. Record source and date. Verify ownership, title, planning, comparables, condition, legal position, finance assumptions and exit.

Release rule

No sensitive address, planning intelligence or investor introduction leaves the system until fee protection and human approval are in place.
